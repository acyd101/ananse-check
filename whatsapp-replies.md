# Ananse Check: WhatsApp Business replies

Save each reply as a **Quick reply** in WhatsApp Business (Settings > Business tools > Quick replies). Type `/` and the shortcut to insert it. Replace the text in [brackets] before you send.

## Greeting message (sent automatically)

> Hello, this is Ananse Check. We are NOT a loan app and we never give loans. Send us the name of a loan app and we'll tell you if the Bank of Ghana has named it as unlicensed. We never ask for your PIN, Ghana Card or money.

## /bad: the app is named as unlicensed

> ❌ [APP NAME] is NOT licensed. The Bank of Ghana named it in Notice [NUMBER] on [DATE]. Please don't install it, and don't give it your contacts or photos. If it is harassing you, call or text 292 (Cyber Security Authority). Full list: [WEBSITE LINK]

## /ok: the app is on the approved list

> ✅ [APP NAME] is on the Bank of Ghana's list of approved digital lending apps. Still read the interest and fees carefully before you borrow.

## /notlisted: the app is on neither list

> ⚠️ [APP NAME] is not on the Bank of Ghana's approved list, and it isn't named in its unlicensed notices yet. That does NOT mean it is safe. Ask the lender for its Bank of Ghana licence, or call 292 before you install it.

## /spell: you can't tell which app they mean

> Can you send a screenshot of the app's name from the Play Store, or spell it out? Many fake apps copy real names.

## /harassed: the person is being threatened

> I'm sorry this is happening. You are not alone, and threats like this are illegal. Report it to the Cyber Security Authority: call or text 292, WhatsApp 0501603111, or email report@csa.gov.gh. Keep screenshots of the messages. Don't pay money to anyone who calls themselves a "recovery agent" without proof.

## /story: asking for a story (only after you've helped them)

> Thank you. Would you be willing to share what happened, for our campaign? We will NOT use your name or number. You can say no, and it changes nothing.

## House rules

- Never ask for a PIN, Ghana Card number, MoMo number or money.
- Never forward someone's messages or number to anyone.
- Before you use a story, get a clear "yes", and remove names and places that identify the person.
- If someone sounds in danger or very distressed, give them 292 first.

## Counting (for the Letter of Intent)

Each day, add one row per question to `pilot-tally.csv`. Label chats in WhatsApp too: Bad / OK / Not listed / Harassed / Story.
