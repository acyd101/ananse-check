Is this loan app licensed?

Type the name of a loan app. We check it against the Bank of Ghana's public notices.

Being harassed by a loan app?

Call or text the Cyber Security Authority on 292, or email report@csa.gov.gh. You can also report unlicensed lenders to the Bank of Ghana at digitalcredit@bog.gov.gh.

Before you install any loan app

Do not give a loan app access to your contacts or photos. Google Play bans personal loan apps from asking for them.
